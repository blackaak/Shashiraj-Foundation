Questions families ask

Still deciding?
Here's what helps.

01 What are the day care timings? +

Day care runs morning to evening, with a structured routine, meals and snacks, and indoor and outdoor activities through the day.

02 Which therapies do you offer? +

Autism treatment programmes, ABA therapy, behaviour therapy, cognitive behaviour therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, learning disability support and early intervention.

03 Is the hostel supervised at night? +

Yes — the residential hostel has 24×7 care and supervision, CCTV surveillance and security, regular health check-ups and daily living assistance.

04 What does senior care include? +

A 24×7 care attendant, nutritious vegetarian meals, an in-house clinic with doctor visits, physiotherapy and homeopathy, yoga and meditation, indoor games and a library, housekeeping and laundry, and round-the-clock security.

05 Can we visit before deciding? +

Please do. Call either number and we'll arrange a visit at the Anand Vihar or Shahdara centre so you can meet the team first.
